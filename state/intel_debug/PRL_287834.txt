Company Profile
PRL is a hydro-skimming reﬁnery incorporated in Pakistan as a public limited company in
May 1960. PRL is engaged in the production and sale of petroleum products. PRL operates
as a subsidiary of Pakistan State Oil Company Limited (PSO), which is the largest oil
marketing company in Pakistan. PRL’s shares are publicly traded on the Pakistan Stock
Exchange Limited.

The reﬁnery is strategically located in Karachi, with a designed throughput capacity of
50,000 barrels per day. The major units in reﬁnery complex are Crude Distillation Unit,
Hydrotreating Unit, Platformer Unit, and Isomerization Unit.

Directors’ Review
The Board of Directors is pleased to present their review report along with the unaudited
condensed interim ﬁnancial information for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

During the quarter under review, ongoing regional conﬂicts continued to disrupt global oil
markets, resulting in signiﬁcant volatility in crude oil and petroleum product prices. The
closure of key shipping routes further constrained crude oil availability and led to increased
freight and insurance costs. In response to these challenging market conditions, the
Government introduced a daily rolling pricing mechanism for High-Speed Diesel (HSD) and
Motor Spirit (MS) to facilitate effective management of petroleum product supplies and
enable a timelier response to ﬂuctuations in international oil prices. Amid these challenges,
the Company recorded its highest-ever quarterly proﬁt of Rs. 13.97 billion during the current
period, compared to Rs. 1.02 billion in the corresponding period last year. Furthermore, it
ensured uninterrupted operations through continued procurement of crude oil, helping
maintain petroleum product supplies and mitigate the risk of fuel shortages in the Country.

In September 2026, the Company successfully executed the revised Upgrade Agreement with
Inter State Gas Systems (Private) Limited (ISGS) under the revised Pakistan Oil Reﬁning Policy
for Upgradation of Existing / Brownﬁeld Reﬁneries, 2023 (as amended in February 2024 and
August 2026). The new policy promises incremental incentives and certain other beneﬁts that
are expected to facilitate the much needed impetus to the efforts to upgrade the Country’s
reﬁning sector.

The signing of the Upgrade Agreement demonstrates the Company’s commitment to the
Reﬁnery, Expansion and Upgrade Project (REUP). The Board has approved a phased
approach to execute the REUP to ensure compliance with the requirements of the Upgrade
Agreement and the Reﬁning Policy in the stipulated timeframe. The Company has
commenced work on this phased approach starting with revalidation of Front End
Engineering Design (FEED) study through technical consultants. Parallel efforts are continuing
on securing ﬁnancial close through the most cost-effective ﬁnancing options available,
including interaction with potential strategic investors.

Health, Safety, Environment and Quality (HSEQ) continued to remain an area of priority and the
Reﬁnery remained compliant with all applicable HSEQ standards. During the period under
review, the Reﬁnery safely completed 12.3 million man hours without any Lost Time Injury (LTI).

The Board of Directors would like to acknowledge and place on record the contributions of all
stakeholders for their continued support, including the Government of Pakistan.

       February 2024 and August 2026) on September 10, 2026. Reﬁneries importing new Plant, Machinery &
       Equipment (PME) for the Upgrade Project will be allowed to withdraw a maximum of 27.5% (capped limit) of
       the total project cost from the Reﬁnery Upgradation Account wherein incremental incentives at the rate of
       2.5% on HSD and 10% on MS will be deposited for a period of seven years from the date of signing of
       Upgrade Agreement and opening of Reﬁnery Upgradation Account. To be eligible for the incentives
       provided in the new Policy of 2026, the reﬁneries were required to enter into an Upgrade Agreement with
       M/s Inter State Gas Systems (Private) Limited (ISGS), within 45 days from the notiﬁcation date of the
       amended policy. The Company successfully executed the revised Upgrade Agreement with ISGS on
       September 24, 2026.

       The new Policy of 2026 amended following provisions of the old policy:

       - a ﬁve year project completion period from signing of Upgrade Agreement and opening of Reﬁnery
         Upgradation Account, with a maximum one year cure period subject to reduction of 1% in the capped
         limit of the project cost. A further one year extension in the cure period at the Federal Government's
         discretion on case-to-case basis;

       - surrender of all collected / accrued incentives under the old Reﬁnery Policy, 2023, with eligibility for a
         fresh seven years incentive period from the date of signing of Upgrade Agreement and opening of
         Reﬁnery Upgradation Account;

       - an additional incentive of 0.5% for each year saved over and above the capped limit, if the project is
         operationalized within three years;

       - reimbursement through IFEM of disallowed sales tax relating to reﬁnery operations for FY 2025-26 and
         until expiry of the Upgrade Agreement;

       - sales tax exemption on certain equipment and materials used in the Upgrade Project;

       - permission to maintain onshore foreign currency account(s) which may be funded through export
         proceeds;

       - introduction of a policy consistency clause regarding policy framework including incentives thereunder.

       The incremental incentives under the new Policy of 2026 will be recognised after the Final Investment
       Decision (FID) on the project.
